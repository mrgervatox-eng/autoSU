How the number is made

The phone reports acceleration along its vertical axis (z, with gravity removed). We take the root-mean-square (RMS) of the last 3 seconds and divide by FULL_SCALE = 3 m/s², capped at 1:

index = min(1, RMS ÷ 3). Still ≈ 0, a firm tap or shake ≈ 1. The dashboard’s limit for a holding area is 0.6 (RMS ≈ 1.8 m/s²).

Confidence = (how much of the 3 s window is filled) × (how fast the sensor reports, 20 Hz or more counts as full). Under 0.6 the validator will not trust it.

No GPS and no microphone are used. Needs HTTPS (see the tunnel steps) and, on iPhone, the permission prompt after you tap Start.
